# 0001: Product category

**ID:** PD-01  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-10-23  
**Stage:** 1

## Context
We need one clear line for what the product is. It sets the landing-page wording and what the prototype has to prove.

## Options
- **Decision workspace:** A place to check a home, test the real cost, compare homes and track questions and answers until you decide.
- **Property report:** Enter an address, get a one-off report. Simpler, but many cheap competitors already do it.
- **Buyer checking tool:** Buyers only. A clearer niche, but it drops renters, against the equal-weight decision.

## Recommendation
Decision workspace.

## Decision
Decision workspace, described in plain consumer language.

## Consequences
Landing copy and the prototype must show the whole journey (check, cost, compare, ask, decide), not just a report. A one-off report alone is not the product.
