# 0006: Prototype centrepiece

**ID:** PD-06  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-10-23  
**Stage:** 1

## Context
The clickable prototype (the first public showcase) needs a defined scope.

## Options
- **Both stories in full:** Buyer service-charge story and renter water-bill story, each from address search to decision, same quality.
- **Buyer story first:** A demo sooner; renters look secondary at first.
- **Tour of all screens:** Shows breadth, proves less.

## Recommendation
Both stories in full.

## Decision
Both stories in full, to the same standard (doc 03 §4).

## Consequences
Stage 2 sample data covers four homes; the prototype isn't launched publicly until both stories are complete.
