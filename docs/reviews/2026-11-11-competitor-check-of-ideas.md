# Competitor check of the doc 05 ideas, 11 Nov 2026

**Purpose:** test whether each idea in [doc 05](../product/05-ideas-and-product-thinking.md) goes clearly beyond what UK products publicly offer (doc 05, question 10). **Method:** web searches and the products' own public pages, checked 11 Nov 2026, starting from doc 02 §3 and `.design/prototype/research/related-products.md`.

**How far to trust each claim:** **[F]** = seen on the product's own fetched page; **[S]** = seen only in a search result, not verified on the page. "Not found" is not proof of absence, and an unreadable page is not evidence of anything. Features and prices were not checked in paid accounts. Several results were small hobby or AI-built sites; they are noted as signals, not established competitors. Competitors' claims are recorded as claims, not facts about property or law (as in doc 02 §3).

## Biggest real gaps
1. **Questions from gaps, tracked per home, for renters and buyers equally (c with h).** Closest: HomeBuyerCheck's paid AI questions and CheckNext's buyer checklist; neither tracks a question to its answer on the home or serves renters. Doc 02 §3 differentiator 1.
2. **"Go and check in person" built from the limits of the data (h).** Every checklist found was generic.
3. **A move-in checklist dated from the real move date and tailored by tenure (b with e and f).** Existing lists are fixed timelines; SlothMove is a paid sending service.
4. **One watch feed per saved home that turns changes into evidence or questions (n).** Each alert type exists separately.
5. **Travel cost inside one sourced monthly total per home (k).** Narrower than it looked: Nestmate overlaps.

## Not yet verified
A portal's "Where can I live?" routine tool; a small app's "day in the life" claim; Doorwise's features (store page unreadable); a portal's ChatGPT app (no primary source); the Environment Agency's replacement sign-up service; pricing and licences of the MCP servers; Locrating (not re-checked).

## Products to consider adding to doc 02 §3
HouseCheckup, Nestmate, Jitty, Rightmove (ChatGPT app, Shared Lists, Ask Rightmove) and the UK property MCP servers, once their claims are checked on their own pages.
